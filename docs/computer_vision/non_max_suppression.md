
# NON MAXIMUM SUPPRESSION & INTERSECTION OVER UNION

## Definition of NMS

Non maximum suppression is a technique used to remove redundant or overlapping bounding boxes that may arise from object detection algorithm

## Definition of IoU

Intersection over Union (IoU) tells us the percentage of overlap between the ground truth bounding box and the prediction bounding box

In case of NMS, we find IoU between two prediction bounding boxes
